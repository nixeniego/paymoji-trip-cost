How the numbers are worked out

Three parts, all shown in the result so you can check them.

Local prices for each day

Three meals, two coffees, a few rides and extras like entry tickets, plus a night's stay. Each travel style has its own price list per country.

Your bank's fees on top

A foreign transaction fee on card spending, plus your bank's fee and the local ATM fee every time you take out cash. Stays booked online count too.

Today's exchange rate

Everything is converted into your home currency at the day's market rate, so the total matches what you would see on Google.

Questions

About travel costs, bank fees and paying in Southeast Asia.

How much does a trip to Vietnam cost per day?

It depends on how you travel. With the prices in this calculator, a budget day comes to about 670,000 VND, a mid range day about 1.8 million VND and a comfort day about 5 million VND, including a place to stay. Pick your days and currency above to see it in your own money.

How much does a trip to the Philippines cost per day?

With the prices in this calculator, a budget day is about 2,600 PHP, a mid range day about 6,400 PHP and a comfort day about 16,200 PHP, including a place to stay. Island hopping tours and flights between islands come on top.

How much does a trip to Bali cost per day?

Choose Indonesia above. With the prices in this calculator, a budget day is about 685,000 IDR, a mid range day about 2 million IDR and a comfort day about 5.5 million IDR, including a place to stay.

What fees does my bank charge when I spend abroad?

Many cards add a foreign transaction fee, often around 3% of every payment. Taking out cash usually costs twice: your own bank's fee for using a foreign ATM, and the local ATM's fee. Some cards charge none of these, so check your bank's fee page and enter your numbers under Your card and bank fees.

How much are ATM fees in Vietnam, the Philippines and Indonesia?

They vary by bank and change often. In the Philippines many ATMs charge foreign cards around 250 PHP per withdrawal. The calculator starts at 50,000 VND in Vietnam and 35,000 IDR in Indonesia, and you can change any of them. The ATM shows its fee on screen before you confirm.

How do people pay in Vietnam, the Philippines and Bali?

Locals pay for a lot of everyday things by scanning a QR code: VietQR in Vietnam, QR Ph in the Philippines and QRIS in Indonesia. Visitors usually cannot use these without a local bank account. PayMoji lets you scan the same codes with money topped up from your own card.

Do I need cash in Southeast Asia?

Some, for small stalls and markets. The more you pay by QR, the less cash you carry and the fewer ATM withdrawals you pay for. Move the cash slider in the calculator to see how much that changes your fees.

Where do the prices come from?

They are estimates of typical prices for each travel style, not live data, so treat the result as a planning number. Exchange rates update every day from a market rate feed.
